CP1 · About

Tester, author, web designer, runner.

I've been testing software since 2009. I started on games at SEGA and have spent most of the years since in finance and insurance, where a bug costs real people real money.

I wrote a book about testing for the people who work alongside testers. In the evenings I build websites for small businesses as Little Oceans. At weekends I run ultra-marathons, ideally over mountains.

I'm organised, I notice details, and I believe in Oxford commas.
